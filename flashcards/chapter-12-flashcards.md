## Chapter 12: Full AB-731 Exam Simulation and 45-Minute Test-Taking Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | AB-731 pacing target: 45-50 questions in 45 minutes | Roughly 55-60 seconds per question. Exam tip: aim to finish with 5-8 minutes left for a final weighted review pass. |
| 2 | Flag-and-return technique — when to use it | Any question taking longer than ~90 seconds: submit your best guess, flag it, move on. Return only during final review time. |
| 3 | Domain weight review rule | Allocate final review minutes proportionally to published domain weight — e.g., a 35-40% domain deserves roughly 2x the review time of a 15-20% domain. |
| 4 | Multiple-response-style scenarios on AB-731 | Never presented as Select TWO/THREE — always single-best-answer. If two actions are both needed, the correct option bundles them into one choice. |
| 5 | Governance framing — what the exam expects | Tie decisions to policy, risk, and accountability, not just cost or preference. Last-minute review priority for compliance-heavy domains. |
| 6 | Tool-selection matrix strategy | First eliminate options violating any hard constraint (compliance, budget cap), then compare survivors against the scenario's stated top priority. |
| 7 | Adoption metrics — what to review last | Know the difference between usage/activation metrics (are people using it) vs. outcome metrics (did it achieve business value) — exam distinguishes these. |
| 8 | Online-proctored exam requirement unique vs. test center | Webcam room scan before start. Both formats require photo ID, clear workspace, and NDA acceptance. |
| 9 | Certification renewal after passing AB-731 | Free annual renewal via a short Microsoft Learn online assessment, completed within the eligibility window before the 1-year expiration date. |
| 10 | Elimination technique for scenario questions | Cross out any option with a disqualifying word (deprecated, non-compliant, over-budget) before comparing the remaining plausible answers. |
| 11 | Final 10-minute review checklist order | 1) Revisit all flagged items 2) Re-check highest-weight domain answers 3) Re-check lowest-weight domain only if time remains. |
| 12 | Time-per-question math check | 45 minutes / 45 questions = 1.0 min/question baseline; 45 minutes / 50 questions = 0.9 min/question. Exam tip: know your exact question count on test day to recalibrate pace. |
