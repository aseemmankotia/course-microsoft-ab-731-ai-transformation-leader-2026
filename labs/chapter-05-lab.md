# Chapter 5 Lab: Microsoft 365 Copilot and Role/Department Experiences

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: given 4 department scenarios, select the most appropriate Microsoft 365 Copilot or role-based experience and justify the choice

## Key takeaway

Choose Microsoft 365 Copilot or a role-based Copilot experience when the need is general productivity or role-specific augmentation grounded in existing organizational content — not custom workflow logic.
