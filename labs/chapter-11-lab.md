# Chapter 11 Lab: Process Redesign, Adoption Metrics, and Scaling from Pilot to Enterprise

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: map a described 6-step customer-service escalation process to a Copilot/agent-redesigned version and define 2 adoption metrics plus 2 impact metrics to track

## Key takeaway

Redesign the process itself, not just add a tool, and require both adoption and business-impact metrics against a pre-pilot baseline before scaling enterprise-wide.
