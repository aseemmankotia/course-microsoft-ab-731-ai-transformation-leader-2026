# Chapter 10 Lab: Standing Up a Copilot Center of Excellence and Governance Guardrails

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: design a lightweight governance policy and CoE charter (roles, review cadence, escalation path) for a 3,000-employee company

## Key takeaway

Match the CoE governance model — centralized or federated — to the organization's structure, and staff it cross-functionally rather than treating it as an IT-only function.
