# Chapter 9 Lab: Assessing Readiness and Leading Change Management

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: sketch a Copilot adoption and change-management plan for a 500-person division moving from pilot to department-wide rollout

## Key takeaway

Assess readiness across data, culture, skills, and sponsorship before sequencing a pilot-to-enterprise rollout, and treat skilling and resistance management as ongoing programs.
