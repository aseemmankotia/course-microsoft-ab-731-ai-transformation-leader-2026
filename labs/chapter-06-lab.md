# Chapter 6 Lab: Copilot Chat, Business Chat, and Copilot Studio for Custom Agents

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: apply the build vs buy vs adopt framework to 3 business scenarios and choose the correct Microsoft AI approach for each

## Key takeaway

Use a build (Copilot Studio) vs buy (pre-built agent) vs adopt (existing Copilot) framework, and route every custom agent — no matter how simple to build — through governance review.
