# Chapter 1 Lab: AI Transformation Opportunities Across Business Functions

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: review 6 mini business-function vignettes and classify each as an automation, augmentation, or transformation opportunity, then justify the classification in 2-3 bullets

## Key takeaway

Leaders find AI value by recognizing whether a function's pain point calls for automation, augmentation, or transformation — not by defaulting to the same solution everywhere.
