# Chapter 8 Lab: Copilot Across the Ecosystem: Grounding, Data Protection, and Licensing

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: review a rollout-request scenario and identify which data-protection and licensing questions the leader must ask before approving

## Key takeaway

Leaders must understand grounding and data protection conceptually, and treat ecosystem Copilots (Dynamics 365, Security, GitHub) as distinct licensing decisions rather than automatic bundle inclusions.
