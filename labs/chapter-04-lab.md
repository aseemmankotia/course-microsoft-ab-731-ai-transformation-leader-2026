# Chapter 4 Lab: Responsible AI Governance, Risk, and Regulatory Awareness at the Organizational Level

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-think exercise: sketch a one-page lightweight AI governance policy covering ownership, risk tiers, and escalation paths for a mid-size organization

## Key takeaway

Responsible AI governance, risk, and regulatory awareness are leadership accountabilities that must be built into organizational policy, not delegated entirely to technical teams.
