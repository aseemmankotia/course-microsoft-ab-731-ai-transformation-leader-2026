## Chapter 1: AI Transformation Opportunities Across Business Functions — Practice Questions

**Q1.** A retail company's customer service team handles 50,000 tickets/month. 70% are password resets, order-status checks, and return-policy questions, with a 24-hour average response time. Leadership wants the strongest first AI transformation opportunity. Which is BEST?

A) Deploy a generative AI assistant to draft/deflect the high-volume FAQ-type tickets
B) Use generative AI to produce marketing content for the same team
C) Replace all agents with autonomous AI voice bots, including for complex escalations
D) Build a predictive analytics model for inventory forecasting

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: A**

High-volume, repetitive, low-complexity requests with clear data patterns are the textbook signal for genuine AI transformation with fast, measurable ROI.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Marketing content generation doesn't address the customer service function's actual pain point.
- Full replacement including complex escalations overreaches — human judgment is still needed there, making this a hype-driven overreach distractor.
- Inventory forecasting belongs to operations/supply chain, not customer service.

> ⚠️ Frequently missed on the exam — read the options carefully.

</details>

**Q2.** Sales reps spend 60% of their time researching prospects and manually drafting outreach emails; deal complexity and relationship trust remain the primary sales drivers. Which is the BEST AI opportunity to pursue first?

A) A fully autonomous AI agent that negotiates and closes deals without rep involvement
B) A generative AI assistant that summarizes prospect research and drafts outreach emails for rep review
C) AI-generated compensation plans for the sales team
D) A predictive churn model for existing customers

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: B**

Research summarization and drafting assistance directly targets a validated, repetitive knowledge-work pain point while keeping humans in the trust-critical parts of the sales process.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Autonomous deal-closing is a hype-driven overreach — relationship and trust work in sales stays human.
- Compensation plan generation is unrelated to the stated research/drafting pain point.
- Churn prediction serves customer success/retention, not the sales research bottleneck described.

> ⚠️ Frequently missed on the exam — read the options carefully.

</details>

**Q3.** An HR director proposes a generative AI 'resume-writing bot' for external candidates as the company's flagship AI initiative. No internal pain point has been defined, executive sponsorship is minimal, and data quality is unverified. Which signal MOST clearly indicates this is a hype-driven pilot rather than a genuine transformation opportunity?

A) A high volume of resumes exists in the applicant tracking system
B) Resume text data is technically available
C) The company already has an HRIS system that could integrate with the tool
D) There is no defined internal pain point and no strong stakeholder appetite

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: D**

Genuine transformation requires a validated internal pain point plus stakeholder sponsorship; their absence is the defining hype-project red flag.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Volume alone is a positive signal only when tied to an internal problem — here it's external and unmoored from any stated pain point.
- Data availability is one of three readiness signals but insufficient on its own without sponsorship or a defined problem.
- Integration capability is an enabler, not evidence for or against genuine opportunity.

> ⚠️ Frequently missed on the exam — read the options carefully.

</details>

**Q4.** A finance team manually writes narrative explanations for monthly budget variances after the numbers are already reconciled. Which generative AI capability BEST maps to this pain point?

A) Summarization and narrative drafting of variance commentary
B) Image generation for financial dashboards
C) Voice synthesis for earnings call scripts
D) Code generation for ETL data pipelines

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: A**

The pain point is turning reconciled data into written narrative — a direct match to the summarization/drafting capability category.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Image generation has no relevance to variance reporting.
- Voice synthesis addresses a different output format not described in the scenario.
- Code generation supports engineering/ETL work, not narrative reporting on already-reconciled figures.

</details>

**Q5.** An operations leader wants to reduce time spent drafting Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) documents from unstructured incident reports. Which generative AI capability is the best fit?

A) Predictive maintenance forecasting on equipment sensors
B) Image classification of equipment defects
C) Real-time anomaly detection on sensor streams
D) Drafting and summarizing structured SOP documents from unstructured incident data

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: D**

Converting unstructured incident narratives into structured documents is a direct drafting/summarization use case.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Predictive maintenance is a forecasting capability, not a generative drafting task.
- Image classification is a computer-vision capability unrelated to document drafting.
- Anomaly detection is a monitoring capability, not a generative content task.

</details>

**Q6.** Per the AI-readiness signal framework, which combination of three signals BEST indicates a function is ripe for generative AI transformation?

A) High executive sponsorship, legacy hardware, and a low volume of unique tasks
B) Large IT budget, complex regulatory environment, and low executive interest
C) High employee turnover, minimal data, and ambiguous process ownership
D) Repetitive knowledge work, sufficient quality data availability, and strong stakeholder appetite

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: D**

These are the three core readiness signals: repetitive knowledge work, data availability, and stakeholder appetite/sponsorship.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Legacy hardware and low task volume contradict the repetitive/high-volume driver of readiness.
- Low executive interest and heavy regulatory complexity are negative signals, not readiness indicators.
- Minimal data and ambiguous ownership directly contradict the data-availability and sponsorship signals.

**Q7.** A marketing team uses generative AI to draft 20 ad copy variations per campaign, and a human reviewer edits and approves each one before publishing. Which category BEST classifies this use case?

A) Automation
B) Augmentation
C) Transformation
D) Elimination

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: B**

A human remains in the loop reviewing and editing AI-drafted output — the defining characteristic of augmentation.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Automation implies the task executes without required human review, which is not the case here.
- Transformation implies redesign of the process or business model, not just draft assistance within the existing workflow.
- 'Elimination' is not a recognized category in this framework.

> ⚠️ Frequently missed on the exam — read the options carefully.

</details>

**Q8.** Which statement BEST distinguishes 'augmentation' from 'transformation' in generative AI business value framing?

A) Augmentation eliminates the human role entirely, while transformation keeps humans fully involved
B) Augmentation enhances an existing workflow with AI assistance; transformation redesigns the process or business model itself
C) Augmentation applies only to customer-facing functions; transformation applies only to back-office functions
D) Augmentation requires more data than transformation

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: B**

Augmentation improves output within an unchanged workflow; transformation changes the underlying process or business model.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- This reverses the actual definitions of automation and augmentation.
- Neither category is restricted to a specific type of function.
- Data volume is not a defining factor distinguishing the two categories.

</details>
