## Chapter 3 Cheat Sheet: Business Case, ROI & AI Strategy Alignment

### Business Case — 4 Required Components
| Component | Must Include |
|---|---|
| Cost | Licensing, compute/usage, integration, training |
| Benefit | Hard ROI + soft/qualitative benefits, clearly labeled separately |
| Risk | Accuracy/hallucination, privacy, governance/compliance, change management |
| Timeline | Phased rollout with expected benefit-realization milestones |

**Exam rule:** A business case missing any one of these four = incomplete, regardless of how strong the cost/benefit data looks.

### Hard ROI vs. Soft Benefits
| Type | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hard ROI | Directly measurable in time/cost/currency | Handle time ↓, cost savings ($), escalations ↓ |
| Soft/Qualitative | Difficult to monetize directly | Employee satisfaction, confidence scores, sentiment |

**Rule:** Never blend hard and soft benefits into a single dollar figure — label separately for credibility.

### TCO — 5 Categories (never "licensing only")
1. Licensing fees
2. Compute/API usage costs (variable)
3. Integration & change management
4. Ongoing governance/compliance monitoring
5. Training

### KPI/OKR Alignment
- Pick AI metrics that **directly map** to the existing OKR outcome.
- OKR: "Reduce resolution time 20%" → correct metric = AI-assisted resolution time reduction vs. baseline.
- Wrong metrics: license counts, training completion %, prompt volume (these are adoption/readiness, not outcome).

### #1 ROI Measurement Pitfall
**Measuring adoption/usage instead of impact.**
- Adoption = logins, prompts sent, licenses activated (leading indicator)
- Impact = cost, time, quality outcome change (lagging/true value indicator)
- Scenario pattern: High adoption + flat impact metrics → **pause expansion**, do not scale based on adoption alone.

### Executive Communication — Avoid Overselling
1. Use your **own pilot data**, not vendor best-case marketing, for projected outcome ranges.
2. Explicitly disclose **known model limitations**.
3. Define what **human oversight** remains (no full-automation promises without evidence).

### Quick Decision Rules
- Business case missing risk section → incomplete, reject/return for revision.
- Metric proposed = adoption rate only → insufficient, request outcome metric.
- Pitch claims full automation on aggressive timeline → red flag, overselling.
- TCO estimate = licensing only → incomplete, missing 4 other categories.
