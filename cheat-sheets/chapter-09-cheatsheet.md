## Chapter 9 Cheat Sheet: Readiness & Change Management

### 5 Readiness Dimensions
| Dimension | Failure Signal |
|---|---|
| Data | Poor model output quality |
| Culture | Distrust, fear, silence on adoption |
| Skills | Users can't complete tasks with tool |
| Leadership sponsorship | Passive approval, no visible modeling |
| Infrastructure/licensing | Access errors, seat/quota shortages |

### ADKAR Model (order matters)
| Stage | Meaning | Failure Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Understands why change is happening | Confusion about purpose |
| Desire | Personally motivated to engage | Compliance without buy-in, fear-driven resistance |
| Knowledge | Knows how to use it | Confusion on steps/features |
| Ability | Can apply it confidently in real work | Trained but not using it, distrust of output |
| Reinforcement | Sustains behavior post-adoption | Usage regresses over time, no follow-up comms |

**Exam trap:** "100% training complete but no usage" → Ability or Desire gap, NOT Knowledge gap.

### Rollout Sequencing (never skip a stage)
1. Pilot (single team, contained risk, validate use case)
2. Single department (test scaled process)
3. Additional departments (confirm repeatability)
4. Enterprise-wide (apply proven playbook)

**Pilot selection criteria:** engaged local leader + repeatable, well-defined use case > department size or budget.

### Resistance Patterns & Mitigation
| Resistance | Root Cause | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| "AI will replace my job" | Fear (Desire gap) | Transparent role-evolution messaging, leadership reassurance, employee involvement |
| "I don't trust the output" | Ability gap | Supervised practice, verification workflows, visible correction examples |
| Silent non-adoption despite approval | Passive sponsorship | Require visible leader modeling + recurring mentions |

### Sponsorship
- Active sponsorship (visible use, communication, reinforcement) is the #1 driver of adoption velocity — approval alone is passive and insufficient.

### Communication Cadence
- Recurring, multi-channel (updates, FAQs, success stories) across the full rollout timeline.
- Single kickoff message with no follow-up → awareness decay + usage regression.

### Skilling Program Design
- Role-based training (tailored to actual task usage) > one-size-fits-all.
- Champions network: embedded peer power-users driving in-team reinforcement and localized troubleshooting — distinct from centralized help desk (technical tickets only).
