## Chapter 12 Cheat Sheet: Full Exam Simulation & 45-Minute Strategy

### Pacing Table
| Total Questions | Total Time | Pace/Question | Buffer for Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45 | 45 min | ~60 sec | 5-8 min |
| 50 | 45 min | ~54 sec | 3-5 min |

**Rule:** if a question exceeds 90 seconds, flag your best guess and move on.

### Domain Weight → Review Time Allocation
| Domain Weight Range | Relative Review Priority |
|---|---|
| 35-40% | Highest — review first, longest |
| 30-35% | High — review second |
| 25-30% | Lowest priority — review only if time remains |

### Flag-and-Return Workflow
1. Read question once, attempt answer within pace budget.
2. Unsure? Select best guess now (never leave blank).
3. Flag it for review.
4. Continue — do not exceed pace budget on any single item.
5. In final 5-10 min, revisit flagged items in weight-priority order.

### Single-Best-Answer Strategy (No Multi-Select Exists)
- Every question has exactly ONE correct option.
- If a scenario seems to need two actions, look for the bundled option ("Do X AND Y").
- Eliminate options violating any hard constraint (compliance, budget, deprecated tool) before comparing survivors.

### Last-Minute Review Priorities
| Topic | What to Confirm |
|---|---|
| Governance framing | Answers tie to policy/risk/accountability, not just cost |
| Tool-selection matrix | Eliminate constraint violations first, then compare priority fit |
| Adoption metrics | Distinguish usage metrics vs. outcome/value metrics |

- Renew free via short Microsoft Learn online assessment.
- Complete within the eligibility window before expiration — do not wait until after expiration.
